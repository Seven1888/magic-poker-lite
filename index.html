 HOW TO PLAY

RULES

  1. Choose a small blind and bring 100 small blinds to the table.
  2. Play preflop, flop, turn and river. CHECK or CALL; BET or RAISE using half pot, full pot or ALL IN.
  3. No top-ups at the table. Leave after a completed hand to cash out remaining chips.

A simplified Hold’em prototype. Shared cards and alternative opponent hole cards are prepared before the hand starts. There are no jokers. Special showdown hands can earn a Jackpot bonus.

Payout rules ⓘ

Royal Flush pays 200 × BB, Straight Flush 50 × BB and Four of a Kind 20 × BB. BB is the table big blind. Your hole cards must help form the special hand, and you must win at showdown. The board alone does not qualify. Awards are never paid after a fold. Bonuses are paid from your personal special pool.

Bonus amounts are fixed multiples of the big blind. Bonus progress carries between hands and tables and is saved on this device.
