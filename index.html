 HOW TO PLAY

RULES

  1. Both players receive two hole cards. Blinds alternate each hand. Small blind acts first preflop; big blind acts first after the flop.
  2. The board is revealed as three flop cards, one turn and one river. Bet in each of the four rounds.
  3. Check when no bet is due. Fold or call when facing a bet. Bet or raise when available, with one raise per street.
  4. The bar previews opponent action odds. After your move, one response is drawn from those odds. It is not your win chance.
  5. A fold ends the hand. At showdown, compare the best five cards from each player’s hole cards and the board. Ties split the pot.

A simplified Hold’em prototype. Optional starting-hand redraws apply only while dealing. There are no jokers. Special showdown hands can earn a Jackpot bonus.

Payout rules ⓘ

Royal Flush pays 200 × BET, Straight Flush 50 × BET and Four of a Kind 20 × BET. BET is the table big blind. Your best hand may use zero, one or two hole cards, and you do not need to win the pot. Awards are paid at showdown only, never after a fold. No pot fee is deducted from the bonus.

These are fixed hand bonuses, not a progressive prize pool.
